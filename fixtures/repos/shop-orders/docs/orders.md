# Orders (EVALUATION FIXTURE)

## Checkout
- Customers place an order with one or more lines. Stock is reserved at checkout.
- **One coupon per order.** Coupons are either a percentage or a fixed amount.
- Tax is 8% of the discounted subtotal.

## Refunds
- Support agents can refund an order **within 30 days** of purchase.
- Partial refunds are not supported yet.
- A refunded order's stock is returned to inventory.

## Emails
- A confirmation email is sent when an order is placed.
